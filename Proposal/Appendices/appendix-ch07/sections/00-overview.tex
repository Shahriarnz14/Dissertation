\ChSevenStarSection{Supplementary Material}
 

Shared reconstruction methods and extraction prompts are provided in Chapter~\ref{chap:ch05} and Appendix~\ref{app:ch05}.
The forecasting sensitivity and full survival results are presented in Sections~\ref{ch07:sec:shared-sensitivity-results} and~\ref{ch07:apd:full_survival}.

This supplementary material provides detailed information organized as follows:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Appendix~\ref{ch07:apd:prompts}}: Prompting strategies for zero-shot and few-shot forecasting.
    \item \textbf{Appendix~\ref{ch07:apd:encoder-forecasting}}: Technical details of encoder-based forecasting methods, including MLP-head and masking approaches.
    \item \textbf{Appendix~\ref{ch07:apd:encoder-survival}}: Survival modeling framework using language model embeddings and the hyperparameters used.
    \item \textbf{Appendix~\ref{ch07:apd:full_tables}}: Complete forecasting performance tables for all models on DeepSeek-R1 and Llama-3.3-70B annotations.
    \item \textbf{Appendix~\ref{ch07:apd:additional_insights}}: Additional insights into forecasting results.
    \item \textbf{Appendix~\ref{ch07:apd:open_source}}: Analysis of open-source models to address data leakage concerns.
    \item \textbf{Appendix~\ref{ch07:apd:beyond_pmoa}}: Generalizability beyond PubMed case reports: comparison with other clinical sources.
    \item \textbf{Appendix~\ref{ch07:apd:broader_impact}}: Broader societal impact and ethical considerations.
\end{itemize}
